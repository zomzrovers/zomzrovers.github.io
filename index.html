Aegis

American Express CodeStreet · Jul – Aug 2026

The question we picked: if AI agents can spend money and call tools for you, what stops one from going rogue? Aegis is a fail-closed policy engine between the agents and everything they touch. Every action is checked for permissions, separation of duties, and spend and velocity limits. If it isn't sure, the answer is no.

The bug that cost us the most sleep was double spending. Two agents could both pass a budget check before either one was recorded. We fixed it with atomic cap decrements on SQLite in WAL mode, then hammered it until it held.

Anila

Platinum Jubilee Innovation Hackathon · IIT Kharagpur × Oil India · May – Jun 2026

Oil companies report their methane emissions, but those reports rarely match what's actually leaking. Anila checks the reported numbers against aerial measurements. A three-pass drone survey feeds a Gaussian plume inversion that traces emissions back to individual pieces of equipment, with honest uncertainty bands. When two sources sit too close to tell apart, it says so instead of guessing.

A knapsack optimiser then decides where abatement money should go first, and a hash-chained ledger makes the whole audit trail tamper-evident.

<1%asset-level bias
